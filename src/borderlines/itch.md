## Description

Every empire had its edges. Can you find them?

You get a year and a realm: the Roman Empire in 1 BC, say, or Australia in 1914. The map has no modern borders, only coastlines and rivers. Paint the land the realm ruled, press Submit, and see how close you came: what you got right, where you spilled over, and what you missed.

**How it works**

- Drag to paint. For big realms, draw the outline and use Fill.
- Stuck? Hints show the region or the capital, for a few points.
- Every day brings five new realms, from easy to hard. Share your score as a little row of squares.
- Practice mode gives you random realms whenever you like.

Each realm ends with a short note on what was really going on that year.

**Part of Homo Ludens**

Borderlines is No. 1 in [Homo Ludens](https://homoludens.oalpcakir.workers.dev/), a small collection of history games you can finish over a coffee. The other games (running the Dutch East India Company, tracing a grocer's receipt from 1850 London to a Cuban sugar estate) are on the site.
