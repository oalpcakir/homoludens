About

Homo Ludens is a small, independent collection of history games, made in Rotterdam: from a five-minute daily map puzzle to an evening at the helm of the Dutch East India Company or a detective's desk in 1850 London. Each game takes one historical idea and turns it into something you can play. The name comes from Johan Huizinga's 1938 book, which argued that play is older than culture.

Spotted a mistake? Every game has a way to report it, and corrections are welcome.

Come and talk history, share your results and hear about new games first on the Homo Ludens Discord.
