About

I make these in Rotterdam. Most of them start with something I was reading: Sidney Mintz on sugar turned into A Cup of Tea, and the traders at the table in Tulip 7 are real people Anne Goldgar found in the Dutch archives. I try to turn each one into a game you can finish in one sitting, and then show you what actually happened. The name comes from Johan Huizinga's 1938 book Homo Ludens, about how old play is.

If I got something wrong, please tell me. Every game has a feedback button, and I do read them and fix things.

There's also a Discord, where people post their scores and new games show up first.
