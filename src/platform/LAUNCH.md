## itch.io devlog (post on Tulip 7's page, and link it from the others)

**Title:** Tulip 7 is out, and the other five games are here too

Tulip 7 is a short card game about the tulip mania of 1636–37. You sit at a tavern table in Amsterdam with three
traders and take tulip papers one at a time. Seven different tulips fill your tulip book; a second copy of any tulip
burns your evening. Over eight evenings the price index climbs, and you have to choose when to cash in before the
market breaks in February.

The traders are real people Anne Goldgar found in the Dutch notarial archives, and each plays to his documented role:
a careful young buyer who backed out of a deal, a cooper who knew his flowers, a brewer who kept buying. When the game
ends you get the actual price curve from those weeks, with your own cash-in marked on it.

It takes about fifteen minutes and plays on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

This is the sixth game in Homo Ludens, a small collection of history games I make. The others are now on itch as
well: Kantar (a two-player duel in the Grand Bazaar, with a teacher), Borderlines (a daily map puzzle), Polder
(dikes and floods across seven centuries), A Cup of Tea (a detective game about one grocer's receipt from 1850) and
Retourvloot (two centuries of the Dutch East India Company). They're all in one place here: COLLECTION-LINK

If you find a mistake, please leave a comment. I read them.
