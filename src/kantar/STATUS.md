# Kantar — status (No. 5, added 2026-10-16)

Two-player "I divide, you choose" duel in the Grand Bazaar, against an AI rival. One self-contained page, English (default) and Turkish.

- **Source:** `src/kantar/kantar.html` is the site copy of the standalone v2.7 build (Claude project): same game plus the portal link, history notes in the rules and resume analytics. `build_kantar.py` adds meta/canonical/icon/analytics/feedback and writes `public/kantar/`, the portal plate and `public/og-kantar.png`.
- **Rules:** 24 goods (Saffron 4×5, Silk 5×4, Coffee 7×3, Copper 8×2), 3 face up + 3 face down out of the game, a stall of 5, the divider puts 3 on the scale (2–1 or 3–0), the chooser takes a pan, the caravan (next 3) is visible. Seals once each: Three Pans, Cut. Majority per good scores its value; fewer goods in total +3 (light load). Narh mode: 2+ of a good on the scale −1, each burned good +1 (range 1–7). Compensation coin to the second divider: Classic +1 (ties go to them), Narh +2 (ties to the Saffron leader). Optional teaching round (seals from round 2).
- **AI:** alpha-beta; Master looks two rounds ahead (small noise), Apprentice one round with more noise. Teacher mode uses the Master search from the player's seat. Both use only public information (tests/kantar.test.mjs reshuffles hidden cards and checks no evaluation changes).
- **Balance (Master vs Master, 300–400 bot games):** Classic first/second divider 47/53, Narh 49/46 (5% draws). Master beats Apprentice ~70–72%.
- **Saves:** `kantar-set` (settings), `kantar-lang`, `kantar-game-v04` (game in progress, v5 format), `kantar.stats.v1` (last 500 games), `kantar.record.v1` (summary read by the portal "Your record" strip).
- **Analytics:** `kantar_game_start` {mode, diff, teacher, first}, `kantar_game_end` {+ result, you, rival, uc, cut}, `kantar_resume`, `kantar_stats_open` (every event also carries `lang`); mapped to `game_started` / `game_completed` in posthog_head. The admin page's Kantar tab reads these (`GAME_SQL.kantar` in worker.js).
- **Home-page art:** `ART` in build_kantar.py: "b" (default) = Amadeo Preziosi, *The Grand Bazaar* (19th c., Pera Museum, public domain; original kept as preziosi-grand-bazaar.jpg, cropped to plate-b.jpg / og-b.png), "a" = the game's scale over its own background (plate-a.jpg / og-a.png). The portal caption lives in src/portal/portal.html.
- **CSS:** one stylesheet, cleaned 2026-10-16 (39.9 KB → 31.9 KB, !important 91 → 11) with zero computed-style differences across 36 states and 3 sizes, and pixel-identical screenshots at 5 more widths.
- **Art:** commodity art re-extracted from the approved portrait cards (GrabCut, decontaminated edges, 384² canvases, optically centred), square card back, wax seal, bazaar background (WebP). Sounds are synthesised (Web Audio).
- **Start sheet (2026-10-16):** first visit: what Kantar is in one line and three steps, then **Learn with the teacher** (Apprentice, Classic, teacher on, teaching round, pan preview; the player divides first) or **Play without the teacher**. Saved settings: **Play** with them (named under the button). A match in progress: **Continue your match**. Every option and the full rules sit under *Settings and full rules* (options first, then the rules and history notes). The header's teacher button shows the setting the next game will use.
- **Teacher box:** the recommended move and its main reason in one line; up to three more reasons and the note on the estimates fold out under "n more reasons" (open/closed is kept across moves). Long reason texts were shortened (EN and TR).
- **Speed (2026-10-26): search in a Web Worker.** The engine section of the page (from the `engine:` marker to the sound section: rules, AI, teacher analysis) is pure; the page starts a Worker from a Blob of that same text (still one file, also in the itch.io/CrazyGames packages, which re-create the inline scripts so the text is still in `document.scripts`). `runJob({type:'ai'|'teach'})` returns the move, or the teacher's analysis with reasons as data (`[key, ...values]`); `reasonText()` turns them into EN/TR on the page. The rival's noise is seeded (`seedFor`: game + position + level), so worker and page give identical moves and a reload replays the same rival move. Fallback to the main thread when Worker/Blob is missing, a CSP blocks blob: workers (no CSP is set in `public/_headers` today), the worker errors or does not answer in 10 s. Stale answers (new game, restore, player moved) are dropped; the "is weighing" note shows while the worker runs. Card art is now drawn from blob: URLs instead of 50–75 KB data: URIs in every `<img>` (each redraw used to parse ~1.5 MB of HTML; `draw()` at 4× CPU 86 → 44 ms max).
  Measured at 4× CPU throttling (Chromium, 4 Master games, teacher on, 1366×768): search on the main thread before: AI max 676 ms / p90 398, teacher max 607 / p90 395; after: none. Longest main-thread task during AI turns 886 → 480 ms (p90 468 → 319), during teacher analysis 558 → 358 ms (p90 330 → 236).
