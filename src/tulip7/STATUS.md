# Tulip 7 — status (No. 6, added 2026-10-23)

A push-your-luck card game of the tulip mania, against three AI traders from the archives, at one table in the Menniste Bruyloft, Amsterdam. English. One self-contained page (art inlined, sound synthesised).

- **Source:** `ui.html` + `art.js` + `carpet.b64` + `engine.js` + `chart.js` + `game.js`; `build_tulip7.py` adds the shared head and writes `public/tulip7/` (also `public/og-tulip7.jpg`, the portal plate `public/tulip7/img/portal-plate.jpg`). The portal shot is `src/portal/shots/tulip7.jpg`. Design log (Turkish): `TASARIM.md`.
- **Rules:** 94 cards (tulips 0–12, each number as many times as its value; offsets +2…+10, a ×2; three each of Wijnkoop / Seghsman / Rouwkoop). Take papers or stand up; a duplicate tulip burns the evening; seven different tulips = tulip book (+15, evening ends). Modes: *The winter of 1636* (8 evenings, winnings go on paper at a rising index ×1→×7, crash risk 0/0/0/4/8/12/20/35% before each evening, certain after VIII, all paper settles at 3½%; cash in at the end of each evening for 2½% wine money; a last cash evening at Alkmaar ×2) and *The tavern game* (first to 200).
- **AI (2026-10-26): three personas** (`PERSONAS` in engine.js; seat → persona, not stored in saves). Same rules, no hidden information. *Van Cuyck* (the buyer who backed out over a guarantor): stands at ~1-in-5 duplicate risk, pushes on while holding a Rouwkoop, seals his own evening with Wijnkoop, cashes in at a ≥10% crash risk or right after anyone else cashes in. *De Busscher* (seller, tulip connoisseur): plays for 5 sorts, chances the 7th at 6, Wijnkoops whoever is nearest a book, takes profit at 2.5× his paper's cost or before a 20% risk. *Van Meeckeren* (one of Goldgar's 37 big spenders): plays to a sum (~30), Seghsmans himself on a safe pile, never seals himself, rides the index until the risk looks steep (sells mostly at VII–VIII, caught by the crash in 56% of winters). Each has his own table talk (`TALK` in game.js). The expected-value rule remains for seats without a persona (the human proxy). Bot balance (3,000 games each, human proxy = steady EV player): winter proxy 26.2%, Van Cuyck 23.7%, De Busscher 21.5%, Van Meeckeren 28.6%; tavern game 26.2 / 21.0 / 26.6 / 26.3%. A cautious proxy wins 18%, a greedy one 24%. Who they were and how they play: start screen portraits and the Rules page.
- **Saves:** `tulip7.save.v1` (start of each evening / round; resumes are deterministic; v1 saves from before the personas resume, missing `cost`/`soldE` get safe defaults; the achievement streaks ride along as `ui`), `tulip7.set`, `tulip7.tips`, `tulip7.ach` (14 achievements), `tulip7.record.v1` (summary for the portal's "Your record").
- **Analytics:** `tulip_game_start` {mode}, `tulip_resume`, `tulip_first_draw`, `tulip_evening` {evening, worth}, `tulip_crash` {after, paper}, `tulip_achievement` {id}, `tulip_game_end` {mode, result, rank, worth, rounds, crash_after, achievements}; mapped to the standard names in posthog_head. Admin stats: the Tulip 7 tab (worker.js GAME_SQL.tulip: funnel, modes, evenings, crash, achievements, depth, daily).
- **History notes:** Van Halmael's f12,000 purchase was "goods", not bulbs (corrected); Jan Theunisz probably died 1635–40 (said in the game). Sources: Garber 1989; Goldgar 2007 and *History Today*.

## 2026-10-26: QA, mobile, personas

- **Bugs fixed:** Rules / Achievements / New game opened mid-game did not pause the table, so bots played on underneath and the crash, Alkmaar or end scene could replace the open window (now a "menu" modal pauses the game; Esc or *Back to the table* returns). The start screen opened mid-game had no way back. Floating "+f…" numbers ran off the right edge and caused sideways scroll on phones. Achievement toasts sat under the end-of-game window. On resume `tulip_evening` was sent a second time for the same evening and `tulip_first_draw` again; the "Five times sold" and "Not content with the guarantor" streaks were lost on reload. Modal focus scrolled the page on touch.
- **Mobile:** below 1050 px the controls are a bottom dock (Take / Stand side by side, ≥60 px tall, safe-area padding); phones get a one-row board, a hamburger menu (incl. *Table log*), rivals as a compact strip of three tiles (short name, worth, a status chip that also shows what is on the table), overlapped hand cards, tips above the dock, a smaller side-by-side announcement. Landscape phones (≤520 px tall) put the controls in a right-hand column. Tap any card, board cell, book or danger chip to read its note (nothing hover-only). `viewport-fit=cover`, `touch-action:manipulation`, sound unlocks on the first tap/key and pauses in the background. Keyboard: T take, S stand, H hold, C cash in, 1–4 pick a player, Enter skips an announcement, Esc closes a menu. The site's feedback/Discord pills are lifted above the dock (CSS in ui.html only).
