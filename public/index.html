Homo Ludens

Short history games, from a five-minute daily puzzle to an evening at the helm of the Dutch East India Company or a detective's desk in 1850 London. One mechanic, one idea, and a note on what really happened. Named after Johan Huizinga's 1938 book, which argued that play is older than culture.

About

Homo Ludens is a small, independent collection of history games, made in Rotterdam. Each game takes one historical idea and turns it into something you can play in a few minutes.

Spotted a mistake? Every game has a way to report it, and corrections are welcome.

Come and talk history, share your results and hear about new games first on the Homo Ludens Discord.
